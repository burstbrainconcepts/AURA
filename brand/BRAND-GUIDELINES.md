# AURA brand system

**Product name:** AURA  
**Descriptor:** Private AI Companion  
**Short promise:** Your private intelligence, within reach.

## Visual identity
- Midnight navy: #080B1C (primary background)
- Deep indigo: #111735 (surface)
- Electric violet: #873BFF (primary action)
- Cyan: #19D8E8 (voice, active and live states)
- Soft lilac: #E7C8FF (highlight)
- Primary text: #F3F4FF
- Secondary text: #9AA6C8

## Design principles
1. Premium, calm, minimal and trustworthy.
2. Use violet for intelligence and primary actions; cyan for voice and live activity.
3. Reserve glow for the assistant orb, active voice state and important progress.
4. Keep text contrast high and provide reduced-motion support.
5. Use the same orb mark across mobile, desktop, setup screens and notifications.

## Product naming
Use **AURA** as the app name and **Private AI Companion** as the descriptor. Avoid changing the product name between platforms.
